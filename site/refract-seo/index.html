Refract SEO

Local by default. Refract SEO is a Chrome extension that audits the page you are on, connects your Google Search Console and other data when you choose to, and tells you what to fix first, why traffic moved, and what is worth doing this week. A default audit makes zero network requests.

  • Fix what costs you clicks, first. Fixes are ranked by the traffic they affect, with the code to copy for your framework.
  • Why did traffic drop? Fixed, tested playbooks rank candidate causes by evidence and say what they could not check.
  • Your AI, your keys — or none at all. Chrome's built-in model, a model on your own computer, or your own key for a provider you choose.
  • Private by design. No Refract account, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays in your browser.

How Refract uses Google data

When you connect Google Search Console, Refract asks for read-only access (webmasters.readonly) to show your own search performance, investigate drops and rank fixes. The data is stored only in your browser, is never sent to a Refract server (there is none), and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Refract's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details: privacy policy.
